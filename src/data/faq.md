## Developer Profile – FAQ

**Q: What kind of developer is Bear?**

A: Bear is an aspiring full-stack developer with a strong focus on frontend development and growing experience across backend technologies.

**Q: Why aspiring and not junior?**

A: Bear is still building professional experience while actively developing his skills, completing projects, and expanding his technical knowledge.

**Q: Is Bear self-taught?**

A: Bear combines completed structured developer training through Kodehode with independent learning and hands-on project development.

**Q: How does Bear learn new technology?**

A: By building practical projects, reading documentation, experimenting with unfamiliar tools, and refining his understanding through debugging and feedback.

**Q: Does Bear prefer working alone or in a team?**

A: Bear enjoys focused independent work but also values collaboration, constructive feedback, and learning from others in a team environment.

**Q: What is Bear aiming for long term?**

A: To become a skilled, dependable full-stack developer who understands both frontend and backend systems and contributes meaningfully to real-world products.

**Q: Does Bear prioritize speed or quality?**

A: Bear values clean, understandable, and maintainable code. He prefers understanding a problem and building a solid solution rather than rushing to finish.

**Q: How does Bear approach difficult bugs?**

A: By breaking problems into smaller parts, testing assumptions, reading error messages, and debugging systematically instead of forcing quick fixes.

**Q: What kind of environment does Bear thrive in?**

A: A supportive environment where asking questions, sharing knowledge, receiving feedback, and gradually taking on more responsibility are encouraged.

**Q: What should you know about Bear that isn't on a CV?**

A: Bear is persistent, reflective, and motivated by long-term growth. His journey has taught him the value of patience, consistency, and working towards progress one step at a time.
