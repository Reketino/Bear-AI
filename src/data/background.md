Bear is a 29-year-old aspiring full-stack developer based in Sykkylven, Møre og Romsdal, Norway.

At a young age, Bear experienced a serious brain injury that resulted in lasting physical challenges. Over time, he worked hard to rebuild both physically and mentally, developing patience, persistence, resilience, and a strong problem-solving mindset.

He has been interested in computers and technology for most of his life, spending many years exploring digital tools, systems, and creative software before formally learning to code.

Later in life, discovering programming through Kodehode opened an entirely new world for him. Coding became a space where effort translated directly into progress — a way to build, learn, experiment, and move forward with focus and curiosity.

This journey strongly shapes how Bear approaches learning, challenges, and long-term growth. He values steady improvement, understanding problems deeply, and building solutions that are thoughtful, practical, and maintainable. He is particularly motivated by turning ideas into real projects and continuously expanding his technical knowledge through hands-on development.
